STEP 01

Open Extensions & Enable Developer Mode

Open Chrome and navigate to the extensions panel. Enter chrome://extensions in the address bar, then toggle the "Developer mode" switch in the top-right corner of the page.

STEP 02

Click "Load unpacked" in the upper-left corner and select the extracted pruwear-extension folder from your downloads.
